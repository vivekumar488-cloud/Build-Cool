03 / AUDIT CHECKLIST

What to collect before making a decision

A useful preliminary audit starts with reliable building information.

Building information

  • Roof area, type, age, condition, leaks and drainage.
  • Monthly electricity bills for at least 12 months, if available.
  • Cooling equipment, operating hours, occupancy and comfort complaints.
  • Roof access, shading, insulation and planned maintenance.

Before approving a retrofit

  • Inspect the roof and repair leaks or substrate issues first.
  • Request product datasheets and installation specifications.
  • Compare installed cost, maintenance, warranties and expected service life.
  • Measure energy use and indoor conditions before and after installation where practical.
