ABOUT BUILD COOL

Start with the building
before the equipment.

Build Cool is an early-stage concept exploring practical ways to reduce unwanted heat gain, support seasonal comfort and make building-energy choices easier to understand.

Roof surfaces, insulation, air leakage, windows, shading, climate and building use all influence energy performance. Our goal is to help people explore options before choosing an upgrade.

Build Cool is not currently a certified product or a verified field-tested savings program. Always confirm roof compatibility, product specifications, costs and potential savings with qualified professionals.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Building upgrades depend on the roof, climate and existing systems. Start with the basics, then verify the details for your building.

What is a cool roof?

A cool roof reflects more sunlight and releases absorbed heat more effectively than many conventional dark roofs. It can help reduce roof heat gain, but the benefit depends on climate, roof construction, insulation and building use.

Will a reflective roof automatically lower my electricity bill?

Not automatically. A reflective roof may reduce cooling demand in suitable buildings, especially in hot climates, but the actual bill impact depends on the building and how it is operated. Build Cool's calculator is an illustrative scenario tool, not a savings guarantee.

Does a cool roof make a building colder in winter?

Reflecting sunlight can reduce useful solar heat gain in some winter conditions. Insulation, air sealing, roof design and local weather all matter, so summer benefits and winter trade-offs should be considered together.

Can I apply a coating to any roof?

No. The roof's substrate, condition, slope, drainage and existing waterproofing must be compatible with the selected product. Follow the manufacturer's preparation and installation requirements and consult a qualified roofing professional when needed.

Is Build Cool selling a certified product today?

Build Cool is currently a concept website for exploring building-performance options. The material values and savings percentages shown are representative assumptions, not certified Build Cool product specifications or verified field results.
